# Recommended-feed catalog policy

[中文](CATALOG-POLICY.md) · [Usage](USAGE.en.md)

Independent publications are eligible, including English-language technology, AI, software and information-security publications. Independence, traffic volume and paid partnerships are not prerequisites. An entry should fit the catalog and have a verifiable official homepage and a publisher-confirmed RSS/Atom URL.

Listing is free of charge and non-exclusive. A publisher may limit the entry to its name, official homepage and feed URLs. Additional category, language or description fields must also stay within the agreed scope. Submit or withdraw a request through a repository issue. After verifying the publisher and exact entry, maintainers update or remove it in a subsequent catalog release. A catalog update cannot recall old installed versions or automatically remove subscriptions that users saved themselves.

**An inquiry or homepage link grants no article-use rights.** Confirm the publisher's proposed listing conditions before registering the entry within that scope. A feed URL may be verified through the publisher's publicly linked official feed; we do not guess or generate addresses. URL and format checks neither subscribe nor expand content-use rights.

Permission to list agreed catalog metadata does not grant rights to republish, adapt, summarize, translate, train models on or sublicense articles or images. The plugin's MIT license covers its code, not third-party content. Listing does not require a publisher to grant those content rights; any separate request for such uses must establish its own applicable rights and conditions.

Browsing the catalog neither subscribes nor fetches all sources. The user chooses Preview and check or Subscribe before the selected feed is requested. A user's subscription does not establish publisher permission for subsequent content uses. Catalog changes do not automatically rename, recategorize or replace existing user subscriptions.
